# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Decision-makers at B2B companies (product leads, CTOs, founders of SaaS products, owners of complex internal tools) looking for a freelance to take a messy business workflow and turn it into a working product. Most arrive from Malt or LinkedIn, often on a phone, already holding his name; they scan for who he is, what he does, whether he is senior enough, and how to reach him. French market first, then English and Spanish.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Jean-Charles Barq, freelance Product Engineer. Success is a qualified visitor contacting him, through Malt or a 30-minute Calendly call.

## Positioning

A Product Engineer (6+ years, also Full Stack developer) who bridges business complexity and product clarity: understand the business, design the product, ship it solid. The three steps are the message; "product engineer." is the headline. AI integration is a secondary, deliberately modest specialty, never the lead.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js + next-intl, locales en / fr / es; `hero.headline` is identical in all three.
- "Développeur Full Stack" must stay visible text for French SEO.
- Contact routes: Malt profile (primary) and a Calendly 30-minute call; CV and portfolio live on Notion.

## Brand Commitments

- The a-mate.tech LinkedIn banner is the binding visual reference: dark `#060910` ground, signature blue `#7FC8FF`, huge lowercase bold "product engineer.", vertical mono `a-mate.tech`, mono `[01] [02] [03]` steps.
- Inter + IBM Plex Mono are the brand faces (from the banner).
- Content and colors are fixed; layout may be creative (editorial poster references supplied 2026-09-28).

## Evidence on Hand

- Projects: Scaleway (Sept 2025 – Apr 2026, technical owner of an internal tool), Stockoss, DGAC DSNA-DTI, Moodoow, QB3 (Alyra capstone), a-mate Sports, with logos in `public/images/`.
- No testimonials, client ratings, metrics, rates or availability dates exist yet: never invent them.

## Product Principles

1. The value proposition and the contact route must be readable within seconds, whatever the layout does.
2. Show the method (the three steps), not a list of skills.
3. Senior and product-minded, never junior-CV: fewer, stronger items.
4. Honest claims only.
